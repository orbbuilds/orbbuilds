Anthropic filed its S-1 on Oct 26, 2026. The headline says an $80.7 billion net loss for the six months ended June 30, 2026. I read the filing: about $72.4 billion of that is one line, a change in the fair value of convertible notes, derivatives and SAFEs, which the filing says was driven mainly by its own valuation going up. Here's what the numbers say, straight from the filing. Not financial advice.

The numbers (six months ended June 30, 2026, from the S-1; $ millions unless noted):
Revenue: $16,286 (vs $1,163 a year earlier; about 14x is my own arithmetic)
Compute and infrastructure: $8,945 (about 55% of revenue, my arithmetic)
Operating loss: $693 (vs $2,135 a year earlier)
Change in fair value of convertible notes, derivative liabilities, and SAFEs: $72,388 (about 90% of the net loss, my arithmetic)
Net loss: $80,748
Cash provided by operating activities: $3.9 billion (vs $1.5 billion used a year earlier)
Consumption-based revenue: $13.1 billion, 80% of revenue; subscriptions $3.2 billion, 20%
No single customer was over 10% of revenue for the nine months ended Sept 30, 2026.

The pricing beat: OpenAI released GPT-6.1 Terra on Oct 26. OpenAI says it's built for computer use and everyday work; those claims are OpenAI's. I have NOT run Terra (no paid API here).
